**Nothing in this directory is edited.** These are the author's files as
received. Errors found in them are recorded in `docs/ISSUES.md` and left
standing in the text. Only the author corrects them.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Bioleaching_Models_v8.docx` | The sequential process write-up, version 8: enzyme harvest, tetrathionate to thiosulfate, leaching. Adds two interventions: pump-limited oxygen and Poisson gold occurrence |
| `Bioleaching Modelling Optimisation.docx` | Optimisation of the leach vessel: crush size, thiosulfate charge, pulp density, their interactions, and staged re-leaching |

## Read this before using them

- **Their stage numbers are not the wiki's.** In these documents, Stage 1 is
  enzyme production, Stage 2 is tetrathionate to thiosulfate, and Stage 3 is
  leaching. On the wiki, stage 1 makes thiosulfate, stage 2 leaches, and
  stage 3 is GolB capture.
- **Authority is not yet decided.** AGENTS.md section 4 names
  `Bioleaching Modelling - Summary.docx` as the authority for stages 1 and 2
  (decision of 2026-09-19). Whether these files supersede it is for the
  team to decide.
- **The optimised design changes the capture feed.** The optimisation
  document recommends 0.094 M thiosulfate, 30 % w/v pulp, 0.5 mm crush, run
  as seven 6 h stages, at 8.77 mg/L of gold per stage. The baseline v8 runs
  use 10 % w/v pulp and report 36.6 % (ammonia) and 19.69 % (no ammonia)
  of gold leached at 24 h.
